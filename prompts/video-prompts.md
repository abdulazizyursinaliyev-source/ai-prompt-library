# Video Prompts (Google Veo / Gemini)

## Formula

[Cinematography] + [Subject] + [Action] + [Context] + [Style & Ambiance]

- Cinematography: camera move (dolly, tracking, crane, POV, close-up)
- Subject: the main focus
- Action: what it's doing
- Context: the setting
- Style & Ambiance: mood, lighting, grade

Clips are 4, 6, or 8 seconds. For a 15-20s reel, chain 2-3 clips.

## Product reveal

> Close-up tracking shot slowly revealing [PRODUCT] on a clean surface, soft natural light. A hand reaches into frame and interacts with it. Background blurred, warm setting. Style: vibrant commercial advertising, shallow depth of field, warm color grading.

## Unboxing / opening shot

> A hand opens [PRODUCT PACKAGING] in slow motion, contents spilling or revealing naturally. Soft overhead light, neutral background. Style: crisp commercial, satisfying tactile close-up.

## Lifestyle use

> [PERSON] using [PRODUCT] in a bright everyday [SETTING], natural handheld camera movement, candid and authentic mood, warm daylight.

## Timestamp format (multi-shot in one 8s clip)

```
[00:00-00:04] description.
[00:04-00:08] description. SFX: sound.
```

## Tips

- Always specify aspect ratio: 9:16 for Reels/TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube
- Name the light source and direction explicitly - it's the biggest realism lever
