# Image Prompts

For product photography, marketing posters, and social media creatives. Works best with Nano Banana / Gemini (product accuracy), Midjourney (clean-plate design), or SDXL.

## Product hero shot

> Studio product photo of [PRODUCT], centered on a clean [COLOR] background, soft diffused lighting from the top-left, subtle shadow beneath the product, high detail, commercial photography style, 4k.

## Lifestyle / in-use shot

> A hand holding [PRODUCT] in a bright, modern [SETTING], natural window light, shallow depth of field, warm and inviting mood, commercial advertising style.

## Marketplace card (Uzum/Wildberries/Ozon)

> Product photo of [PRODUCT] on white background, centered, no shadow distortion, 1-3 word headline text at top in bold sans-serif, 2 spec callouts as small chips in corners, max 4 colors, text never overlapping the product.

## Instagram carousel cover

> Bold, eye-catching product shot of [PRODUCT] with large readable headline text, vibrant [BRAND COLOR] background, flat clean composition, designed for a 1:1 Instagram feed post.

## Rules that keep results usable

- Never ask the model to redesign the product itself (shape/color must stay accurate)
- Keep headline text under 6 words
- Max 4 colors in the whole composition
- Always specify lighting direction - it's the #1 thing that makes AI product shots look fake or real
